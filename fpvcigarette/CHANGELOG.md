# Changelog

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-18)
- Dancer (`/cig dance`): a dancing night elf on a small stage with a chrome pole behind her, in a window that can be
  moved, resized (corner grip) and scaled (mouse wheel, `/cig dance scale`); right-drag turns her; lock makes it
  click-through. Looks: night elf NPC appearances with baked textures (40 outfits per sex, `/cig dance next`), a display
  ID of your choice, or your own character. New textures `pole.tga`, `stage.tga`.

## 0.1.0
- First release: a lit cigarette overlay in first-person view. `/cig` lights it, every further `/cig` takes a drag.
- Flipbook textures (32-bit TGA with alpha, generated by `tools/make_textures.py`): cigarette burning down in 7 stages,
  a 16-frame smoke puff, an additive ember glow.
- Smoke particles (stream from the tip, puff when lit, exhale after a drag), ember flicker/flare, hand sway and turn lag.
- Shown only in first person (`GetCameraZoom`, or a zoom estimate from `CameraZoomIn/Out`); hidden when dead, on a taxi,
  in a vehicle or a cinematic. `/cig always`, `/cig move`, `/cig scale`, `/cig time`.
- Debug log in SavedVariables (`/cig debug`, `/cig log`), client capability probe on login.
